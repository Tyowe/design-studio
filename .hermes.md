- Creative direction
- Strategic decisions
- Final approval
- Client relationship

AI:

- Research
- Analysis
- Ideation
- Creative development
- Design assistance
- Review
- QC assistance
- Revision analysis
- Documentation

---

## Workspace Principles

1. Client objective comes first.
2. Design decisions should have a reason.
3. Research supports design strategy.
4. Creativity must serve communication.
5. Quality control is mandatory.
6. Previous revisions must be preserved.
7. Important creative decisions require human approval.
